01 How I think

The hard part is rarely just the answer.

I like problems where the difficulty is finding the right model, building a reliable way to solve it, and understanding whether the result holds up.

01Model the problem

Find the structure first. Mathematics, probability or optimization before any tool is chosen.

02Build the system

Turn the model into a pipeline, an interface or infrastructure someone else can actually use.

03Measure the outcome

Compare against real baselines, test the assumptions, report what actually improved.
